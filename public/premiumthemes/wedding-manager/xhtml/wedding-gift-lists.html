With so many amazing (and free!) wedding gift lists available it can be hard to know exactly which one would be best for you. Luckily, Wedding Manager has done all the hard work deciphering the different wedding lists for you! Below you will find some of the best award-winning wedding registries in the UK, so whether you’re dreaming of luxury wedding gifts or just want a honeymoon fund, you’re sure to find exactly what you need. Peruse our top choices below and you’ll be building your dream wedding gift list in no time!

  • Group gifting allows for groups of friends or family contribute to one gift together. Great for when you include some pricier gifts!
  • Honeymoon funds allow your guests to contribute cash to spend on your honeymoon. This is a great feature but keep in mind some gift lists only allow you to spend the funds on their partnered resorts or brands.
  • Ring-fence protection means that your gift list provider guarantees that your gift list funds will be deposited outside of their business. Think of this as an insurance that no matter what happens, your money and gifts will always be safe!
